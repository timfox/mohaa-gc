# MOHAA on GameCube: remaining retail gaps

This is a running list, updated with each PR. Each entry says what retail does that the port doesn't yet, and where it shows. Newest changes are at the top of each section.

## Campaign status (sweep of 2026-11-26)

Every retail mission was converted with `tools/mohaa_sd.py` (stages scripts, tags, scriptmodels, maps, worlddl, props, sounds, loading) from main f4daf3c, put on a smoke disc (`build-gamecube-disc.py --probe-menu-newgame --world-render`, `XASH_GC_PROBE_STARTMAP=<map>`), and booted headless in Dolphin for 130 s (MAP_READY plus about 60 to 100 s). Script errors and unbound commands are counted over the whole run. The heap column is the lowest `heap_free` the engine logged during the load and after MAP_READY. Stills are in `media/2026-11-26-campaign-sweep/`.

**All 34 maps boot** (29 on the sweep; m5l3, m6l1c, m6l3a and m6l3c since round 2 item 2). Those four ran out of MEM1 loading the world. m6l1c, m6l3a and m6l3c have more than the 16384 hull-0 nodes the engine's static reserve holds; hull 0 now goes into the unused tail of the static clipnode table (xash3d-gc claude/mohaa-hull0-tail), and coarser collision in `tools/map_flags.txt` (`--simplify-collision --collision-grid 64 --mesh-collision`) leaves the room: clipnodes 30307 → 11738 (m6l1c), 25251 → 15086 (m6l3a), 21203 → 13015 (m6l3c). m5l3's 50647 clipnodes (396 KB) failed to reload; at grid 32 with mesh collision it has 21500 and fits the static table. Rows for these four come from runs one at a time with the round 2 DOL (two Dolphins started in the same second mix their run folders). **m6l2a boots since round 3** (xash3d-gc claude/mohaa-hull0-raw-nodes): its 19127 nodes beside 19856 clipnodes fit neither static table, so hull 0 (149 KB) goes into the world array spill, where the raw BSP nodes were just reclaimed from. The spill reaches 947 KB of its 1 MB once the world DL chunk cache is in, so m6l2a is the tightest map for it. Lowest free heap: 5.9 KB during load, 310.9 KB after MAP_READY.

**Memory headroom.** The heap column above is `heap_free` alone, which drops to 6 to 9 KB at boot on every map but is not the limit: the heap grows into `unclaimed` arena memory. The real headroom is `heap_free + unclaimed`. Its lowest point per map, during load (where) / after MAP_READY, for the tightest maps of the sweep: m1l2a 106 KB (map active, re-measured 2026-11-27; the sweep's 13 KB came from an earlier build) / 336 KB; m3l1b 45 KB / 299 KB; m3l1a 48 KB / 302 KB; m3l3 48 KB / 302 KB; m2l2b 75 KB / 329 KB; m5l1b 80 KB / 334 KB; m1l3b 92 KB (bsp-buffer-release) / 199 KB. Every other map keeps over 110 KB during load. m1l2a was the one to watch. On current main it keeps 82 KB of heap plus 24 KB unclaimed at "map active" (the Zone Engine pool at 93 KB, the map's own pool at 39 KB). The one allocation that still failed during its load was the path-node table (below); it now loads after the map is up. The engine's heap census (`heapcensus`) reads back garbage on this Dolphin build (its table at 0x81800000 is not kept), so it could not name call sites.

**Bound since the sweep** (round 2 item 7): `speed` (a move goes at that pace), `sighttrace`, `istouching`, `earthquake` (screen shake), `doactivate` (fires the trigger's targets), `volumedamage` (damage at the entity), `targetname` (command form), `stopsound` (stops the entity's loops). Accepted and ignored, each for a stated reason: `type_grenade`, `perferredweapon`, `noticescale`, `bulletspread`, `showmenu`/`hidemenu`/`hidemouse`, `cache`, `ammo_grenade`, `stopwatch`, `bsptransition`, `vehicleanim`, `setsize`, `nextdrive`, `turret` (on vehicles). The rows for m1l3b, m2l2c, m2l3, m4l3, m6l3d and m6l3e are from runs with this binding: none reports an unbound command except m4l3's `droptofloor`, which the earlier ones had hidden. The lowest free heap during load is now 6.2 KB (8.7 KB on the sweep): the DOL has grown with rounds 2 and 3. **Round 3 (2026-11-27):** a string used as a listener now resolves to the entity of that targetname, as retail's ScriptVariable::listenerValue does (cabinet.scr, ai.scr and alarm_system.scr hand global/spawner.scr a soldier's `.target` string). Script errors: m3l2 100 → 5, m5l1a 98 → 12, m6l2a 100 → 30. m5l1b's cabinet soldiers (t1984-t1990) were still waiting for a grunt-pool slot when cabinet.scr looked them up; since round 4 a name lookup brings a waiting soldier in (an unnamed soldier gives way, else the named one farthest from every player, 2500+ units off, goes back to the waiting list keeping its name, keys and target), and m5l1b's script errors go 100 -> 14 (what was left, weather.scr's farplane on a NULL `$world`, is fixed since: `$world` is a point the binding makes on first use, and `farplane`, `farplane_color`, `farplane_cull` and `farplane_bias` are kept for the script to read back; the GameCube renderer draws no MOHAA distance fog). m5l1b now runs with 0 script errors. `droptofloor` and `cansee` (clear line, optional fov cone and distance) are bound; m4l3 and m6l2a report no unbound command. Lowest free heap during load in these runs: 7.5 to 9.2 KB.

## Missions and scripts
- **Bound entities' angles are local** (2026-11-27). Setting `.angles` on an entity bound to another now sets its angles against its parent, as MOHAA's do, and setting `.origin` keeps the new place against the parent (it was snapped back to the bind offset each frame). spotlight.scr pitches each searchlight's bulb with `self.bulb.angles = (pitch 0 0)` on a base that yaws toward the moving aim point. With a temporary print in a staged copy of spotlight.scr, m1l2b's spotlight 2 sweeps in Dolphin: its aim point flies its path and the base turns from 162° to 251° yaw over 17 s, the bulb pitching between -15° and -46° (`media/2026-11-27-spotlight-sweep/spotlight2-aim-log.txt`). The towers drew bare: the searchlight models were never baked, the client's lean studio list skipped `models/miscobj/`, and the base is the map's own script_model, written as an info_target, which `model` refused. Since 2026-11-27 the three searchlight TIKIs are baked static for the seven maps that place a searchlight (m1l2a, m1l2b, m4l2, m4l3, m6l1c, m6l2a, m6l2b: 70 KB of disc each, about 19 KB resident once loaded), xash3d-gc claude/mohaa-lean-miscobj draws and skins `models/miscobj/`, and a map's info_target script_model takes its scripted model with every bodygroup shown: the lamp stands on its tower (`media/2026-11-27-searchlight-models/tower-before-after.jpg`). The "lamp" fixed in the top-right corner was not a searchlight: it is retail's held-item icon (global/items.scr draws `item_radio_explosive` there with huddraw once the player has the radio explosives), so it is right. The dynamic `light`/`lightRadius` beam is still not drawn.
- **Searchlights (spotlight.scr) run** (2026-11-27). `$(entity)` returned NULL: the VM only looked strings up by name. spotlight.scr's search loop runs `while (... isalive $(self.spotter))` with `self.spotter` an actor, so every searchlight ended on its first frame and deleted its flare under the still-running corona thread ("set on a removed entity", NIL vector math). `$(entity)` is now that entity. In Dolphin: m1l2b script errors 44 → 0, m4l2 48 → 0, m1l2a 43 → 19 (`media/2026-11-27-spotlights/`); m1l2b's four spotters now man their MG42s ("spot1owner mans the MG42 t355", where all four crews waited before). The lights themselves stand still in the stills; whether the beam sweeps is not checked yet.
- **Script points cost no edict** (2026-11-27). Named invisible points (`script_origin`, `info_waypoint`, `info_splinepath`, `info_vehiclepoint`, `info_notnull`, `info_null` with no model) now go to a `maps/<map>.points` sidecar instead of the .map, and the game holds them in one table: scripts find them by `$name` (arrays too), read and set their origin and angles, follow their targets (patrol chains, followpath, drive), bind and move them, and read their `#` keys. They load on the first frame after the map is up, before the level script's main. m3l1a's 1104 points had been 1326 info_targets of which the edict budget spawned 187; now all its logic entities spawn (222 of 222). In Dolphin: m3l1a script errors 28 → 5; m1l3b 45 → 39; m1l2a, m1l2b and m4l2 unchanged (their errors are spotlight.scr's, next), with m1l2b's patrols walking point chains ("patroller03 patrols to t262 ... t268 ... t267") and m1l2a's three MG42 crews manning their guns as before. Memory, loaded after MAP_READY: m3l1a 56.6 KB (437 KB free at that point), m1l3b 11.6 KB, m4l2 9.6 KB, m1l2b 8.9 KB, m1l2a 4.9 KB (40 bytes a point plus its names). Heap at "map active" is unchanged on every map tried (`media/2026-11-27-script-points/log-excerpt.txt`). Maps must be converted again to get the sidecar; until then they keep their info_targets.
- **m3l1a script errors: 69 → 28** (2026-11-27). The Higgins boats 2 to 6 were left out of the map: the converter keeps a name only when the scripts can reach it, and it saw `$("higgins" + local.m + "_move")` as reaching nothing, since the number sits in the middle of the name. A name built from two quoted pieces around a number now stays (`tools/bsp19_to_map.py`), so the boats' doors, clips, sprays, origins and movers are in the map, and the script's 25 failed binds became 19 "too many bound entities": the cutscene table held 64 entities, and the landing binds six boats of about 13 parts each. It now holds 128. In Dolphin the rangers ride the boat in over the water (`media/2026-11-27-m3l1a-script-names/`, left: before, right: after, both at 60 s). The 28 left are named points the engine never spawns: m3l1a writes 1326 info_targets (926 of them `info_waypoint` ranger paths) and its edict budget keeps 187, so `$shingle_objective`, `$higgins1_coxswain_spot` and the `$mg42_target_*_origin` arrays are NULL.
- **The campaign player can walk** (2026-11-26, xash3d-gc claude/mohaa-probe-sticks). The HL1 intro-tram code had pinned the player to m1l2a's *12 brush, and single-slot world-render boots skipped server PM_Move. The player's maxspeed is also set to 220 when nothing else has set it.
- **m1l2a door hint: verified on screen.** In Dolphin, USE reaches `door_locked` (80 units away), the trigger fires, the retail locked-door rattle plays, and the hint thread's `iprintlnbold` draws "Press USE to open doors. If you cannot open the door, you will be able to hear the handle rattle." under the compass for 5 s, wrapped to two lines (`media/2026-11-26-m1l2a-walk-use/m1l2a-hint-1.jpg`, `-2.jpg`).
- **m1l1 2nd-ranger truck animations** (`truck_idle_captain` and the like) can't be applied. Mission actors wear DoD player bodies, not TIKI skeletons, so the passengers stand in their idle pose.
- **Path nodes on large maps** (2026-11-27). m1l2a's 1104 path nodes (46 KB) did not fit while the map's load buffers held the heap, so its walks went in straight lines. A node table that does not fit at load is now loaded again on the first frame after the map is up, before the level script's main runs: "1104 path nodes from maps/m1l2a.nodes, 3070 links (9347 walk tests)". Heap at "map active" is unchanged (82 KB free plus 24 KB unclaimed); 336 KB is free when the nodes load (`media/2026-11-27-m1l2a-path-nodes/`).
- **Music** needs the CD-sourced loose music (`--cd`) or `--music-source`. A Paks-only install has none.
- **Dialogue** aliases missing from map sound tables, e.g. m1l1 `dfr_m1l1_add01` and `truck1_run`.
- Bound since the last update: `loc_convert_string`, and `getboundkey1`/`getboundkey2` (pad buttons). The m1l2b hint read "NILNOT BOUNDNIL"; it now prints "NOT BOUND" as retail does for keys the pad has no button for.

## AI (see MOHAA_AI_ASSESSMENT_2026-11-26.md)
- **Perception:** `sight`, `hearing` and `fov` are bound now (2026-11-26): per-grunt sight range, hearing radius and field of view in GruntLook. Still missing: `noticescale`, `enemysharerange` (alerting nearby allies), and a curious state that turns toward a noise before it attacks.
- **Zones:** `leash`, `tether`, `mindist` and `maxdist` are bound (2026-11-26). An actor stops at its leash edge, fights from `maxdist`, and backs off inside `mindist`. `fixedleash` is accepted, since the leash centre is already fixed. Still missing: `tether` only centres the leash where the entity was when the command ran; it doesn't follow a moving entity.
- **Cover** (2026-11-26): a fighting actor runs to the nearest free cover path node (MOHAA flags DUCK, COVER, CRATE or CONCEALMENT) within 520 units and fights from there; `attackplayer` actors charge instead. Seen in Dolphin on m1l1 ("GRUNT 36 takes cover at -2963 -1754").
- **Accuracy** (2026-11-27, round 5): `accuracy N` (command, map key, spawn key) is bound. A soldier's hit chance is scaled by N/50 (0.2x to 2x; unset keeps the difficulty curve), on top of the campaign's difficulty scale. Map soldiers keep their retail `accuracy` (m1l2a has 20, 25, 32, 70 and 100). On m1l2a, two measurements: in front of the t736 MG42 nest (crew accuracy 100), the grunts' hits per shot went from 0.27 to 0.53 (`media/2026-11-27-grunt-accuracy/`); 150 units from the accuracy-20 riflemen at `lock_enemy`, they went from 0.29 (2.1 shots, 0.6 hits a second over the run, accuracy ignored) to 0.07 (1.4 shots, 0.1 hits). Those are small samples (about 15 to 20 shots a run), so read them as direction, not rates: most retail soldiers carry accuracy 20 to 32, so the campaign overall gets easier, and MG42 crews (100) get deadlier. The grunt row is now 99 bytes. Lowest free heap in that run: 8.1 KB during load, 336.7 KB after MAP_READY.
- **Damage to the player** (2026-11-26): grunt shots are now scaled by difficulty. Two rifle hits (51 each) used to kill the campaign player; now each hit is about 15 / 23 / 31 on easy / medium / hard. Their hit chance in the campaign is now scaled by difficulty too (x0.35 / 0.5 / 0.7 on easy / medium / hard).
- **The campaign player had no side** (fixed 2026-11-26). On the single-player rules the player joins no team, so grunts never treated them as an enemy. They now count as Allies in MOHAA's campaign.
- **Soldiers don't fight each other.** Grunts only target players, so the Allied friendlies and the Germans ignore one another.
- **Weapons and attack styles** (2026-11-26): `gun` and the `.weapon` field pick the DoD weapon a soldier fires, by MOHAA name; `"none"` leaves it unarmed, and an unknown name falls back to its side's rifle. `type_attack runandshoot` charges; `cover`, `turret` and the other styles fight from cover. `startfiring` and `stopfiring` toggle charging on actors and are ignored on turrets. `type_attack turret` / `machinegunner` mans the nearest MG42 (see MG42 crews).
- **MG42 crews** (2026-11-26): `turretweapon_german_mg42` entities are kept (the converter's `mohaa_classname`) and wear the baked `statweapons/mg42_gun` model on m1l2a and m1l2b. A map actor whose target names one is its crew, as is an actor told `type_attack turret` or `machinegunner` within 160 units of one. A crew stays at the gun, watches the gun's arc (`#maxyawoffset`, 60 degrees each side by default), fires at anything in it at any range, in 2-round bursts about every 0.35 s, and never takes cover or charges. Actors keep their map `target`, and `.gun` / `.turret` set to a targetname point at that entity, so spotlight.scr's gunners find their MG42. Seen in Dolphin on m1l2a: "t735 mans the MG42 t736" (and t737, t738); the player, placed in front of the t736 nest, loses most of their health in a few seconds. Since item 3 (2026-11-26):
  - The gun sounds as retail: `tools/ubersound.py` stages `mg42_gun_snd_fire` (MG42Fire5.wav) for every map that places a `turretweapon_german_mg42`, and crews and the gun play that alias (DoD's sound only when a map has none). In Dolphin the alias resolves to `mohaa/weapons/fire/mg42fire5.wav`; smoke discs carry no MOHAA sounds, so the sample itself comes from the SD card.
  - The turret's own commands are bound: `setaimtarget` / `clearaimtarget`, `burstfiresettings`, `maxyawoffset`, `startfiring` / `stopfiring`. With an aim target and `startfiring`, the gun fires on its own in bursts (and now and then hits a player in plain sight).

  - Turretweapons are written first among the map's named points, so the edict budget no longer drops them (m1l2a's t736 and t738 were lost on the sweep's conversion). A soldier that waited for a pool slot keeps its map target and still mans its gun.
  - **The player can man an MG42** (item 6): USE within 80 units of a gun with no live crew mounts it.
