## Result

The retail compass housing and needle are 128x128 TGA assets. The prior converter reduced both to 32x32, which discarded the fine housing marks and produced a visibly coarse ring in the 640x480 gameplay capture. The converter now keeps those static layers at 64x64 while the rotating face and objective arrow remain 32x32/16 frames. The resulting smoke ISO assembled successfully and passed the disc builder's embedded-DOL check.

The first launch attempt exited at Qt initialization with `qt.qpa.xcb: could not connect to display :0`; the live capture tool found no guest window. A second bounded launch reached `MAP_READY m1l1`, but the smoke builder had omitted every `sprites/mohaa` file. The team-picker frame and log are archived under `media/playtests/screenshots/20261124-034121-m1l1-compass-64-static/`; HUD loads fail there, so it did not test the new art. The follow-up with those assets included exposed a fixed-32x32 draw rectangle; see [the 03:45 probe](20261124-034529-m1l1-compass-64-hud.md). The later source-rectangle fix and successful HUD capture are in [the 03:50 probe](20261124-035031-m1l1-compass-64-fixed.md).

The lower-resolution source comparison is available at `/tmp/mohaa-compass-resolution-compare.jpg`. It illustrates source detail loss but is not an in-game result.
